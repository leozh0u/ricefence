# GOAL (fixed for this run)

Build the first real version of ricefence.com, the Rice Fencing Club site: a dark,
cinematic, scroll-scrubbed fencing intro followed by the practical club info.
It must be good enough for Leo to show the other officers without apologising.

Bar: CHECKLIST.md in this folder (checklist mode).

Hard rules
- Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. GSAP + ScrollTrigger from cdnjs, Lenis from jsdelivr.
- Dark fencing palette, Rice navy and grey as accents. No Rice owl logo or official marks.
- No invented facts. Every club fact on the page comes from OwlNest, the Rice catalogue,
  the old club site, or Leo. Unknowns are left out or listed in HANDOVER.md, not guessed.
- Copy follows Leo's voice rules: no em or en dashes, no AI vocabulary, no filler
  subheadings under headings, no punchline fragments, no contrast reframes.
- Stock footage is from Pexels (free licence) and is a placeholder until the club shoot.
- Commits as Leo Zhou <zhouleo2007@gmail.com>, no AI trailers.

Caps: 6 critic rounds.
